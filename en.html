002

Vaccine Museum

Opened on March 7, 2023, at Parque da Ciencia Butantan, the Vaccine Museum was presented as the first museum in Latin America dedicated to the topic. The space occupies Casa Rosa, a 550 m² historic building, and uses interactive experiences to explain immunizer production, the immune system, immune memory, and vaccination.

My contribution: I was responsible for creating and keeping multiple museum activations running, including iPad games about ovoscopy and vaccine production, touch tables to simulate vaccination and infection scenarios, a collective circular table to contain virus spread, and quiz totems with fact-or-fake vaccine questions, answer logic, and user feedback.

Jornal da USP Parque da Ciencia

003

Intacta i2x

Intacta 2 Xtend is a soybean biotechnology platform associated with protection against key crop caterpillars and tolerance to glyphosate and dicamba herbicides. Activations at agribusiness fairs work as technical communication experiences to explain crop management, productivity, and field technology.

My contribution: I developed interactions linked to Intacta's cashback system and a visual decision-making experience where the user needed to contain a pest before it took over the crop field.

Intacta Bayer

004

CCXP Minecraft

CCXP24 took place from December 5 to 8, 2024, in Sao Paulo, as a major pop culture and geek entertainment convention. Warner Bros. Pictures brought an interactive booth with special activations for A Minecraft Movie, connecting the film campaign to a physical fan experience.

My contribution: I was responsible for the interaction data capture and interpretation system. Using a LiDAR designed for indoor mapping, I built a system capable of detecting laser interference within a defined area, calculating the interference position, and sending that data to the interaction in real time.

CCXP Gshow
